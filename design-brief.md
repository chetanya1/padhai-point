# Padhai Point design brief

- Design read: Reassuring contemporary home tuition for Delhi families and college learners who value clarity over pressure.
- Concept spine: The learning desk. School notebooks and technology sit side by side.
- Delivery tier: Editorial website with responsive motion, bespoke CSS/SVG hero artwork, scroll reveals and a moving subject ribbon.
- Animation mode: animated-website requested; image generation was rejected on the current account plan, so a video scrub was not shipped. Lightweight CSS motion is used instead of broken video paths.
- Palette: ink green #172923, botanical white #f6f7f2, leaf #718d67, forest #3e604d.
- Typography: geometric display and system sans body for fast loading.
- Mobile framing: single column under 640px, mobile navigation and responsive subject rows.
- Section plan: split hero, subject index, teaching approach, tutor profile, computer-science band, accordion FAQ and enquiry form.
- Asset plan: bespoke CSS artwork, monogram, vector favicon and social cover. Generated image/video kit unavailable because the account plan rejected image generation.
- SEO: title/meta, location-relevant copy, EducationalOrganization JSON-LD, service-area phrasing without a fabricated street address, semantic headings and responsive layout.
- Contact integrity: no fabricated phone, testimonials, ratings, student results or teaching-experience claims. Owner must configure phone/WhatsApp before launch promotion.
